Who I Am

I am a third-year B.Tech student in Computer Science Engineering (AI & Data Science specialization) at Sanjivani College of Engineering, Kopargaon. My goal is to become an impactful Data Scientist and Software Engineer — someone who can bridge the gap between raw data and intelligent systems.

On top of that, I specialize in end-to-end machine learning workflows using Python, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Pandas, and IBM Cognos Analytics — transforming raw data into actionable predictions.

Beyond academics, I actively build hands-on projects (fraud detection, disease prediction, crop recommendation), pursue industry-recognized IBM certifications, and study Japanese to prepare for technical opportunities in global markets.
